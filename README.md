# Studio-Kimemia
### Official Art Storefront & Curated Exhibition Platform for Ian Arts (@ian._arts)

Welcome to **Studio Kimemia** — the official digital exhibition and luxury storefront showcasing the original fine artworks, hyperrealistic charcoal drawings, and contemporary canvas masterworks of visual artist **Ian Kimemia**.

---

## 🏛️ Project Overview

Studio Kimemia is designed with an editorial, gallery-grade white aesthetic, combining classical fine art presentation with high-performance e-commerce and seamless social discovery.

### ✨ Key Features

- **Fluid Shifting Pieces Exhibition (Hero Moving Gallery):**
  - Continuous 60fps infinite horizontal shifting carousel showcasing artworks framed in modern architectural interiors.
  - Interactive speed controls, pause-on-hover, drag-to-scroll, and quick piece inspection.
  - Live piece indicator and dynamic exhibition curation status.

- **Curated Artwork Catalog & Filter System:**
  - Filtering by *Charcoal & Graphite Realism*, *Acrylic Canvas*, *Contemporary Street Art*, and *Original Masterpieces*.
  - Dual Display Toggle: Switch between "In Room" living space mockups and "Pure Artwork" views with instant hover flip.

- **Collector-Grade E-Commerce Suite:**
  - **Size Selector:** 12×16", 16×20", 18×24", 24×36", and Original Masterpiece.
  - **Material & Framing:** Unframed 310gsm Cotton Rag Paper, Matte Black Gallery Wood Frame, Warm Natural Oak Frame, and Museum Stretched Canvas.
  - **Collector Volume Bundles:** Tiered discounts (Buy 1, Buy 2 Save 15%, Buy 3 Save 20%) with real-time price recalculation.
  - **Live Multi-Currency Converter:** Instant switching between USD ($), EUR (€), GBP (£), and Kenyan Shillings (KSh).
  - **Slide-Out Cart Drawer:** Real-time subtotal, quantity controls, promotional voucher support (`IAN10` for 10% off), and free insured shipping threshold tracker.
  - **Express Simulated Checkout:** 256-bit encrypted simulated order flow supporting Apple Pay, Google Pay, Credit/Debit cards, and M-Pesa with instant invoice generation.

- **Social Proof & Verification:**
  - Loox-style floating real-time collector review toast notifications with verified buyer badges.
  - Authenticity guarantee: 100+ year archival inks, museum glass, and hand-signed Certificates of Authenticity.

- **Instagram Link-in-Bio Integration:**
  - Dedicated mobile-first [`links.html`](links.html) landing page optimized for Instagram profile bios (`@ian._arts`).
  - In-app modal access for seamless mobile engagement.

- **Custom Commissions Studio:**
  - Dedicated commission inquiry form for bespoke portraits, cultural icons, automotive art, and custom canvas sizes.

## 🚀 Deployment & Local Setup

### Local Preview
Open `index.html` or `links.html` directly in any modern browser.

### GitHub Pages Deployment
1. Go to repository **Settings** → **Pages**.
2. Select `main` branch as the Source.
3. Your site will be live at: `https://ianarts9974.github.io/Studio-Kimemia/`
4. Set `https://ianarts9974.github.io/Studio-Kimemia/links.html` as your Instagram bio link!
